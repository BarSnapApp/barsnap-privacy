Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

BarSnap Retail is a free barcode and number scanning application designed to work offline.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize unnecessary collection and processing of personal information.

1. Camera Access

BarSnap Retail requires access to your device's camera to scan barcodes and recognize printed numbers.

Camera images are processed locally on your device. BarSnap Retail does not upload camera images or scanned content to servers operated by the developer.

2. Local Data Storage

BarSnap Retail stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Scanning history and saved barcode information.
  • Images saved through the application's scanning features.
  • Application settings and preferences.

This information is not automatically synchronized with an online account or cloud service operated by BarSnap Retail.

Uninstalling BarSnap Retail may permanently delete locally stored information.

3. Internet Connectivity

BarSnap Retail's core scanning features are designed to work without an internet connection.

The application uses Google ML Kit for barcode scanning and text recognition. While scanning and recognition are performed on the device, Google ML Kit may communicate with Google's services for technical purposes, including diagnostics and service maintenance.

4. Third-Party Technology

BarSnap Retail uses Google ML Kit, a technology provided by Google.

According to Google's documentation, ML Kit may collect technical information such as device characteristics, application information, performance metrics and diagnostic events.

Such information may be transmitted to Google to support the operation, maintenance and improvement of its services.

Learn more in Google's ML Kit data disclosure documentation .

5. Advertising and Accounts

BarSnap Retail does not display advertisements and does not require users to create an account.

BarSnap Retail does not operate its own advertising tracking system.

6. Data Sharing

BarSnap Retail does not intentionally transmit scanned barcode values, recognized text or saved scanning images to servers operated by the developer.

Technical information may be processed by Google through the integrated ML Kit software, as described above.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved scanning history, images and application preferences remain on your device until deleted through available application functions, Android's app-data controls, or application uninstallation.

BarSnap Retail does not maintain an online database containing your scanning history.

8. Children's Privacy

BarSnap Retail is a general-purpose scanning utility and is not specifically designed for children.

The application does not require personal information to create an account.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to BarSnap Retail, its technologies or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page.
