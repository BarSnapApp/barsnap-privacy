Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

BarSnap Retail is a barcode and number scanning application designed for practical retail workflows. Its core features are designed to work without an internet connection.

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection and processing of personal information. This policy explains how the application handles camera access, locally stored content, backups, and third-party technology.

1. Camera and Image Access

BarSnap Retail requests camera access to scan barcodes, recognize printed numbers, and capture images used in the app. You may also choose images using supported device selection features.

Scanning and recognition are performed on the device. The developer does not operate a server to receive your live camera feed, scanned barcode values, or images.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, BarSnap Retail stores information locally, including:

  • Saved barcode values and scanning history.
  • Organized folders, product details, and associated photos.
  • Multiple Scanner archives, images, and related information.
  • App settings and preferences.

BarSnap Retail does not provide a developer-operated account or cloud synchronization service for this content. Some device-level backup services, if enabled, may separately back up app data under your device provider's policies.

3. Checkpoint Backup and Restore

BarSnap Retail includes a Checkpoint feature that allows you to export app data and associated files into a ZIP backup and later restore them, including on another device.

You control where you save or share exported backup files. A Checkpoint ZIP may contain barcode history, product information, photos, and other saved app content. Treat backup files as potentially sensitive and share them only with people or services you trust.

Restoring a Checkpoint may replace existing app data. The app is designed to create a safety checkpoint before restoring; review the in-app instructions before proceeding.

If you store a Checkpoint ZIP with a cloud-storage provider or send it to someone else, that provider or recipient may process the file under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Internet Connectivity and Third-Party Technology

BarSnap Retail has network permissions, although its core scanning features are designed for offline use. Internet permission alone does not mean that scanned content is uploaded.

The application uses Google ML Kit technology for barcode and text recognition. Processing is performed on-device, but Google states that certain ML Kit components may collect or transmit technical information, such as device and app information, performance metrics, or diagnostic events, depending on the component and configuration.

For details, see Google's ML Kit data disclosure documentation .

5. Accounts, Advertising, and Sharing

BarSnap Retail does not require an account and does not display in-app advertisements or operate its own advertising tracking system.

The developer does not operate a service that receives your scanned barcodes, recognized text, saved product photos, or Checkpoint files automatically. You may choose to export or share files using your device's available apps and services.

Third-party SDKs may process limited technical data as described above.

6. Retention and Deletion

Saved data generally remains in the app's local storage until you remove it using available app features, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently delete locally stored content.

Checkpoint ZIP files saved outside the app are not automatically deleted when you uninstall BarSnap Retail. You must manage or delete those copies in their saved locations.

Device backup services may also retain copies according to their own settings.

The developer does not maintain an online database of your scanning history.

7. Children's Privacy

BarSnap Retail is a general-purpose retail utility and is not specifically designed for children. It does not require users to create an account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, its technology, or applicable requirements.

The current version will be available on this page, with the updated date shown above.
